Security & Data Protection Blueprint

Security controls required before real schools or learners can use DSAMS.

Security-first design

🔑 Strong authentication

Unique accounts, secure password hashing, multi-factor authentication, session expiry and account recovery controls.

🧩 Role-based permissions

Separate platform administrator, school administrator, admissions, finance, teacher, parent and read-only roles.

🏫 School data isolation

Every request must be scoped to an authorised school tenant on the server. Never rely on a browser selector to protect records.

🧾 Audit trails

Record sign-ins, access to sensitive records, approvals, edits, exports and administrative actions.

🛡️ Secure infrastructure

HTTPS, secure secrets management, encrypted backups, patching, monitoring, rate limits and tested recovery procedures.

🇰🇪 Privacy compliance

Complete a data-protection impact assessment where required, define lawful purposes, minimise data, manage retention and support data-subject rights under Kenyan law.

Critical security boundary: GitHub Pages can host this static demonstration, but it cannot provide secure server-side login, enforce school-by-school database isolation, safely process payments, or protect live student records on its own. Do not upload real student data, passwords, identity documents or payment details to this demo. A production backend, database, access-control layer, security review and operational policies are required.

Recommended production release gates

  1. Confirm the legal entity/operator and school onboarding authority.
  2. Design and review the tenant-isolated backend and database.
  3. Implement authentication, MFA, role permissions and audit logging server-side.
  4. Complete privacy, child-data and security assessments with qualified Kenyan professionals.
  5. Run penetration testing, backup restoration tests and a limited pilot before wider rollout.
